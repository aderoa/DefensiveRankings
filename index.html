#!/usr/bin/env python3 """ patch_def_stale.py -- do not claim a season the data is not from ========================================================================== Dry by default. --apply writes, and takes a .before-stale backup first. It refuses otherwise. python patch_def_stale.py --file index.html python patch_def_stale.py --file index.html --apply python patch_def_stale.py --file index.html --undo WHY THIS EXISTS, AND IT IS MY MISTAKE patch_def_season.py made the page take the season from the calendar instead of from the sheet's meta row. That was the right fix for the LABEL and the wrong fix for the problem, because the sheet's data is last season's: Defensive_Rankings Season: 2025-26 | Last Updated: 2026-06-14 Player Traditional PerGame Season: 2025-26 | Last Updated: 2026-05-28 Top five on that tab: Wembanyama 64 GP, Holmgren 69, Wallace 77, Thompson 73, Gobert 76. A full season, and no 2026-27 anywhere in the spreadsheet's 36 tabs. So the first patch alone puts "2026-27" above 2025-26 numbers, which is a worse failure than a stale label: a wrong label invites you to check, a confident wrong label does not. WHAT THIS ADDS 1. THE DATA'S OWN DATE DECIDES. "Last Updated" is already parsed out of the meta row; it just was not used for anything. The season that timestamp falls in is the season the data is from, and the page now takes the calendar label ONLY when the data agrees with it. 14 June 2026 falls in 2025-26, so the block stays 2025-26 and the dropdown tells the truth. 2. A BANNER, NOT A CONSOLE LINE. When the live tab is from an earlier season than today's, the page says so above the table, naming the date and the season it actually holds. The first version of this logged a console warning, which is a note to whoever opens devtools -- and the whole failure here is that nobody was told. WHAT IT DOES NOT FIX The sheet. Nothing in this page can produce games that are not in the spreadsheet. The thing to look at is whatever Apps Script writes those tabs: its last successful run was 14 June 2026, the day after the 2025-26 Finals ended, which points at a trigger that stopped for the offseason and was never restarted, or a season constant still reading "2025-26" -- the same pinned- season trap that had gr_daily.py re-fetching a finished NBA season every night. A third possibility worth ruling out by running it once by hand: Apps Script fetches leave Google's own servers, and stats.nba.com refuses datacenter networks, which is exactly why the NBA box score Action had to be replaced with a local fetcher. CRLF is preserved: read and written with newline="".
